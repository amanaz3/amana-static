Documents & Requirements

Q21

What documents are generally required to open a business bank account?

While requirements vary by bank and business activity, you will typically need: Company Trade License, Memorandum of Association (MOA) / Articles of Association, Board Resolution for Bank Signatories, Passport copies of all shareholders and signatories, UAE Residence Visa and Emirates ID (for residents), Proof of Company Address (e.g., tenancy contract, utility bill), Proof of Personal Address for shareholders, Bank statements (company or personal, typically 6 months), Company Business Plan (for some cases).

Q22

I don't have an office space (I use a flexi-desk). Is this a problem?

Not necessarily. Many banks accept the personal address proof of the company manager or signatory for flexi-desk and virtual office setups. We will advise you on the best way to present your case to satisfy bank requirements.

Q23

What if my documents are not in English or Arabic?

All submitted documents must be translated into English or Arabic by a certified legal translation service. We can assist you in arranging this service if needed.

Q24

What is 'MOFA Attestation' and do I need it?

MOFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) attestation is the process of legalizing documents from foreign countries for use in the UAE. It is typically required for the constitutional documents of any corporate shareholder that is based outside the UAE. We can manage this entire process for you through our trusted partners.
